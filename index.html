🌟 How to study smart

First say the answer aloud. Then tap to reveal it. Practise a Studio challenge and try a quiz. Stars are a reward for learning, not for racing through the work.

📋 What this app covers

Based on the supplied Creative Arts class worksheets: music notation, rhythm, instruments, musical terms, voice types, movement, mirroring, festivals and cultures. The general school examination guide does not list a separate Creative Arts syllabus, so these are practice topics rather than a claimed exam paper outline.
